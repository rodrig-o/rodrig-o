# Hi there, I am **Rodrigo Cândido de Oliveira** 👋

I am an **Architect, Urban Planner, and Urban Data Scientist** specializing at the intersection of computational design, geospatial analysis, and regenerative architecture. With a strong foundation in parametric modeling and Python programming, I integrate spatial data science and agentic workflows (using tools like Antigravity and Claude Code) to solve complex territorial and bioclimatic challenges.

My goal is to bridge architectural excellence, urban planning, and agroecology through data-driven, innovative technology.

- 🌐 **Exploring:** Advanced methodologies connecting geoinformatics, algorithmic modeling, and sustainable masterplanning.
- 🎯 **Current Focus:** Developing technology-driven projects that integrate regenerative design, agroforestry, and urban resilience.
- 💡 **Methodology:** Combining BIM, parametric workflows (Rhino/Grasshopper), and spatial relational databases (PostgreSQL/QGIS) for evidence-based design.
